ScamShield AI Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 29, 2026

1. Introduction

ScamShield AI is an AI-powered application designed to help users identify potential scams, phishing messages, and suspicious communications.

2. Information We Process

When you request an analysis, ScamShield AI processes the messages you paste and the screenshots you choose to upload.

This content may contain personal information. Please remove passwords, account numbers, financial credentials, and other sensitive information before submitting content.

3. AI Analysis

Submitted messages and screenshots are transmitted to our backend and Google's Gemini AI service to identify potential scam indicators and generate explanations and safety recommendations.

Google's processing of submitted content is subject to its applicable terms and privacy practices.

4. Subscriptions

ScamShield AI uses RevenueCat to manage subscription access. Depending on your platform, subscription transactions may be processed by Apple or Google.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information processed by the app. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Choices and Privacy Requests

You can choose whether to submit a message or screenshot for analysis. For questions about personal information or privacy requests, contact us using the email address below.

7. Children's Privacy

ScamShield AI is not specifically designed for children. Children should obtain permission from a parent or guardian before using the app.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as ScamShield AI develops. Any updates will be published here.
